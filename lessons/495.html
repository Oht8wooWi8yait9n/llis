NASA Lesson #0495: Systems Engineering, System Safety and R&QA Program Integration, Operational Safety and Performance, Risk Management, and Facility Safety Inspections

Lesson ID: #0495 (Database ID: 495)
Date: 1983-02-28
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

December 9, 1982, the 80 x 120 foot wind tunnel at the Ames Research Center was undergoing commissioning/shakedown testing when Vane Set 5 collapsed destroying the vane set, the fan blades of all six drive motors, and damaging the tunnel itself.

Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Procedures for effective systems engineering on safety issues are lacking.
  2. System safety and R&QA omitted for project design decisions results in design deficiencies and possibly manufacturing defects affecting safety.
  3. The user/operator community does not understand the design constraints on the system imposed by issues of safety and risk management.
  4. Cost control does influence performance, safety and reliability.
  5. Modifications to facility design contained specific configurations that serve to abate previous safety inspection hazards.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Define procedures to ensure effective systems engineering.
  2. Assign review and approval authority to system safety and R&QA on project design decisions.
  3. Involve the user/operator community in the design decision process regarding safety and operational safety issues.
  4. Institute cost control systems for safety and reliability.
  5. Check all aspects of facility design construction and operation against modification.
