NASA Lesson #1249: Orbital Debris Project/Funding Issues

Lesson ID: #1249 (Database ID: 1249)
Date: 2002-06-17
Organization: NASA Headquarters
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Funding cuts threaten the viability of the orbital debris project.

Lesson(s) Learned

Funding cuts threaten to eliminate all effort on maintaining and updating surveillance and modeling of the orbital debris population as early as October 2002.

Recommendation(s)

Reexamine the decision to eliminate this important function, and assure that the core Micro Meteoroid Orbital Debris (MM/OD) effort is continued.

Evidence / Additional Context

Office of Space Flight is seeking to identify all users/stakeholders of the current Orbital Debris Program and identify appropriate program content and long-term Agency funding source(s) to assure NASA retains capability for compliance with Agency Orbital Debris Policy for NASA missions.
