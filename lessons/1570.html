NASA Lesson #1570: In-House vs. Out-of-House Staffing Mix

Lesson ID: #1570 (Database ID: 1570)
Date: 2005-06-05
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

There is no single driving event.

Lesson(s) Learned

The key lesson learned here is that core technical research and associated research support should be maintained within the NASA research cadre and on-site contractors.

Recommendation(s)

All levels of management should recognize and support during all phases of programs and projects the critical importance of this. When programs and projects get into implementation and likely guideline and budget reductions and/or risks need to be addressed, the existence of an in-house core staff to carry on the critical functions of the project becomes key to the eventual successful completion of the work. Cost and resource estimates must plan and take this into account and those making resource and funding decisions must recognize and respect it.
