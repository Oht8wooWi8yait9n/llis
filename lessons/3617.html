NASA Lesson #3617: Performance Measurement Baseline (PMB) Implementation Reporting Expectations on Letter Contract Award

Lesson ID: #3617 (Database ID: 3617)
Date: 2010-09-24
Organization: Marshall Space Flight Center
Program Phase: Formulation
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Topic / Category: Program Management, Acquisition / procurement strategy and planning
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The event driving this lesson learned was a pilot project conducted on the CxP Extravehicular Activity (EVA) Project as part of the Agency's EVM Capability Project.

Lesson(s) Learned

Prime contractors must comply with the EVM System and reporting requirements included in Letter Contract awards and projects should only allow waivers to these requirements due to extreme circumstances. This includes the initialization of the PMB, submission of the CPR and the Integrated Master Schedule (IMS), and conduct of the Integrated Baseline Review (IBR) to the extent practical within 180 days after award (in accordance with the contract EVM clause). Additionally, Projects should strive for timely contract definitization of letter contracts so that a comprehensive and reliable PMB can be established, performance measured and evaluated, and better insight is gained into the contractors and the overall Projects ability to meet the technical/cost/schedule objectives.

Recommendation(s)

Ensure Agency EVM System Description Document (SDD) and best practices clearly discuss application of EVM on prime contracts and major subcontractors to include expectations regarding initializing the PMB and EVM reporting on letter contract awards. EVM Capability team developed training and revisions to appropriate EVM related Handbooks should address EVM requirements and best practices of PMB development under letter contracts.

Evidence / Additional Context

Projects receive EVM reporting from contracts operating under letter contract conditions. Projects are able to assess cost and schedule performance prior to contract definitization. Letter contracts are definitized in accordance with NFS 1843.7005 which refers to FAR 16.603 (Letter contracts definitized within target date of 180 days after date of letter contract or before completion of 40% of the work to be performed, whichever occurs first).

Related Policies & Standards

For additional information, review ANSI/EIA-748, Earned Value Management Systems and NFS 1816.6 Time and Materials, Labor-Hour, and Letter Contracts
