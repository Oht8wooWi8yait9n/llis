NASA Lesson #0038: Undetected GH2 Release in the OPF

Lesson ID: #0038 (Database ID: 38)
Date: 1992-09-18
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

After landing and prior to fuel cell deactivation, PRSD tank pressures normally will increase. The GH2 pressure will vent from the right side of the orbiter when pressures reach approximately 290 psi. This venting has occurred during roll-in of the orbiter to the OPF and could cause a fire if an ignition source is present.

Lesson(s) Learned

Failure to establish controlled venting of PRSD tanks prior to exceeding tank relief pressures, could cause inadvertant venting and danger of fire.

Recommendation(s)

Ensure all OMI's are updated to establish controlled venting at the SLF (or other safe area) after landing and prior to OPF roll-in.
