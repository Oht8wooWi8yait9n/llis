NASA Lesson #1435: Accident Investigations/Procurement of Goods and Services

Lesson ID: #1435 (Database ID: 1435)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

There was concern at NASA over the role of the Columbia Accident Investigation Board's procurement of goods and services through a support contractor. NASA and other government agencies have the ability on occasion to provide services much cheaper. Procurement decisions were often made on the basis of the perception of independence rather than cost efficiency or effectiveness.

Lesson(s) Learned

In general, an independent accident investigation board may use outside contractors on the basis of “independence” even if that function can be provided more effectively by the NASA center or another government agency.

Recommendation(s)

  • Draft language in the Contingency Action Plan (CAP) that enables full use of existing governmental (e.g. FEMA, NTSB, DOD, DOJ) resources for investigation purposes. Services provided to the investigation groups will require 24/7 support at startup with the appropriate organizational functions in place as required.
  • Consult with pre-identified onsite experts to purchase products need (e.g. high resolution camera, recording devices, software system), so (1) the users of the product can be properly trained or a professional can be provided, and (2) the hardware/software can be utilized by that provider at the end of the project.
