NASA Lesson #0589: Utility Vehicle Incident

Lesson ID: #0589 (Database ID: 589)
Date: 1998-06-04
Organization: Marshall Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

On May 16, 1998, MSFC held an quotOpen Housequot for the public. During the event an elderly woman fell from the rear of a NASA owned and operated utility vehicle, hitting her head on the pavement. The visitor was taken to the hospital and fortunately released with no serious injuries. An investigation determined that the woman requested the vehicle driver to take her to another location within the Center. She sat on the rear bed of the vehicle. When the vehicle moved forward she slipped off.

The utility vehicle is a 4 wheel skooter with a small flat bed manufactured by Cushman (see photos below).

Lesson(s) Learned

Vehicles not designed as personnel carriers should not be used for personnel transportation.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Use special vehicles only for intended and designed use.
  2. Assure all drivers of vehicles are trained in proper use of utility vehicles.
