NASA Lesson #1043: Construction Site Safety, Electrical Shock

Lesson ID: #1043 (Database ID: 1043)
Date: 2001-07-26
Organization: Armstrong Flight Research Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

A contractor employee was working on an energized electrical sub-panel.(480v) While removing cover from above the busses the employee dropped the cover shorting out the busses. An arch and flash occurred slightly burning the employee's hands. The main breaker tripped causing an uncontrolled shutdown of the equipment. The interior of the panel was damaged requiring replacement of interior parts.

Lesson(s) Learned

Do not allow work to be performed on energized electrical equipment.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Require Activity Hazard Analysis for all tasks.
  2. Require Utilities outage request/Permit before working on electrical facilities.
  3. Do not allow employees to work on energized equipment. Conduct daily construction-site safety meetings.
  4. Ensure proper training of employees. 5. Ensure employees use proper tools and require proper PPE as well as follow NASA safety processes in place for electrical work.
