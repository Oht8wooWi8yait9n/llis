NASA Lesson #1362: Program and Project Management: Customer Relations

Lesson ID: #1362 (Database ID: 1362)
Date: 2003-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Project, whose main objective was to reduce human error caused incidents and accidents in aviation, was dependent on acquiring highly sensitive information from several of its customers/stakeholders. This was made especially challenging by the increased demands for security and resources placed upon the airline industry by the 9/11/2001 terrorist attacks.

Lesson(s) Learned

In order to build and maintain a trusted and established relationship with its data sources and partners, the Project ensured awareness and sensitivity to their environment by:

  1. Adjusting their information requests/requirements to be compatible with the provider's schedule and resources.
  2. Hiring and consulting people with histories and background experience working with these sources.
The result of these efforts not only maintained the Project's relationship with the industry but strengthened it as the airlines did their best to respond to the Project's original schedule of requests and recommended other sources of information to fill any gaps.

Recommendation(s)

Projects having dependencies on partners that may be extremely sensitive to social/economic/political events should consider:

  1. Defining milestones, in a broad sense, such that their schedule can be adjusted to compliment that of their partners.
  2. Staffing the Project with personnel who have experience working with similar partners and in a similar volatile environment (personnel with direct work experience with the Project's partners may prove to be valuable assets to have).
