Driving Event / Description

The supplier delivered squeegees that tested positive for trace amounts of silicone despite clear specifications prohibiting it. The supplier believed it had met this requirement because no silicone was used in the material used to form the squeegee. However, an investigation determined that the supplier had used a silicone-based release agent to ease squeegee removal from the manufacturing molds.

Lesson(s) Learned

If suppliers do not understand the rationale for customer specifications, they may draw erroneous conclusions about customer needs and constraints that may lead to process control escapes.

Recommendation(s)

Do not rely solely on specifications and other instructions to ensure full supplier compliance with process control requirements, but educate suppliers so they will understand the underlying rationale.

Evidence / Additional Context
