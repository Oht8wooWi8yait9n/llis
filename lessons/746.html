NASA Lesson #0746: Facilities, Mod & Rehab Projects

Lesson ID: #0746 (Database ID: 746)
Date: 2000-01-01
Organization: Glenn Research Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During Mod & Rehab projects, typically people are moved out of the offices. This brings about two situations. First , extra workload on the Project Manager to coordinate the moves, i.e. finding temporary office areas, the moving of the furniture, relocation of telephone numbers and computer drops. This can be a full time task in itself.

Since the people are out of the office it is the perfect time to paint the offices and corridors. This has previously been accomplished with continual maintenance funds. The reality of budget cuts has eliminated this type of effort.

Lesson(s) Learned

  • It becomes a logistics nightmare for a Project Manager to coordinate; locating free space, managing the moving process, telephone and computer drop relocations.
  • The Project Manager can and does lose contact with the Mod/Rehab work.
  • Some Mods & Rehabs have not had the proper cosmetic work and the offices and corridors present a shoddy, unfinished appearance.


Recommendation(s)

  • Create the role of a Move Coordinator within the Project Team.
  • When project requirements are being created, include specifications for any and all necessary cosmetic repairs to the facilities.
