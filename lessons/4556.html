NASA Lesson #4556: Program vs. Center Processes

Lesson ID: #4556 (Database ID: 4556)
Date: 2010-12-16
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Approval
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Programs coming to a Center to execute projects should avoid reinventing processes already in place at the Center. The Constellation Program (CxP) had the Ground Operations Project (GOP) at Kennedy Space Center. CxP invented its own process for business systems documentation in addition to the one the Center already had. This resulted in two different processes for records management: one for the Center and one for the CxP Business Records Template (BRT). Kennedy Space Center (KSC) is governed by NPR 1441.1D, NASA Records Retention Schedules, and KNPR_1440.6, KSC Records Management which requires a business records listing. The CxP developed a "catalog" (i.e. access database) with considerable additional attributes to be managed for Program records. This resulted in Projects having two requirements for records identification, which is an additional effort.

Lesson(s) Learned

Programs should make use of Center processes where possible. The Agency should establish a uniform process and requirements for records management and other processes. In this case, KSC business records documentation (i.e., Kennedy Documented Processes (KDPs) vs. CxP documentation) was the issue. If the Center already has processes in place to perform the work, use the available resources to achieve the work.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Programs doing business at multiple NASA Centers should utilize the processes already established at the Center, which is especially important to ensure integration and standardization.
  2. Use established Agency-wide process for records management and implement it at the start of any new Program with continuous monitoring for any new additional requirements, and ultimately for Program closeout purposes and efficiency./li>
