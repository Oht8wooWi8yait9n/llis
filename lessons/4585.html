NASA Lesson #4585: Standardize Technical Coordination Meeting (TCM) Process Used In Document And Data Reviews

Lesson ID: #4585 (Database ID: 4585)
Date: 2010-12-17
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Approval
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Communications between different offices and contractor personnel, Cross Agency coordination, Organizational Planning
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During the Constellation Program, data and document reviews were conducted inconsistently. Multiple document revisions occurred during four separate informal evaluations prior to the comments being dispositioned. This caused confusion for everyone involved in the review and wasted valuable technical resources. Even though the Constellation Program had an approved formal Technical Coordination Meeting (TCM) process, how the TCM was conducted varied widely depending on who was managing the review (i.e., virtual TCM, email only, telephone only). It was difficult to give direction to Change Package Engineers and often elicited complaints from them.

Lesson(s) Learned

A well-documented process for both Informal and Formal reviews must be established and followed by all those leading the reviews and these reviews should include Technical Coordination Meetings (TCMs).

Recommendation(s)

Programs and Projects must establish and adhere to an approved Informal and Formal Technical Coordination Meeting (TCM) process as part of their review/change management processes.
