NASA Lesson #0330: Debonding Due to Pressure Trapped in Voids with Subsequent Exposure to External Reduced Pressure

Lesson ID: #0330 (Database ID: 330)
Date: 1993-06-14
Organization: Marshall Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

KSC post flight inspections of RSRM-2 (STS 27) showed a 3.5" x 3.5" piece of JPS cork missing from the right hand center field joint at 185 degrees indicating debris. An unbond also existed between the EPDM moisture seal and the extruded cork at this missing location. The missing cork was located at the kevlar band buckle, where the JPS cork interface had been cut to fit over the buckle, creating an unvented void area under the cork.

Lesson(s) Learned

Voids in bond lines can result in debonding if a pressure differential is created between the void and the external environment.

Recommendation(s)

Eliminate voids in bond lines (as between cork insulation and the substrate) where possible; if not possible, provide vent holes at the void site.

Evidence / Additional Context

Engineering assessment indicates that provision of vent holes at known void sites would eliminate the possibility of pressure differential causing a debond.
