NASA Lesson #1191: NASA Photo Archive Damaged by Flood

Lesson ID: #1191 (Database ID: 1191)
Date: 2001-04-21
Organization: Goddard Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Early on Saturday, April 21, 2001, a pipe in the secondary water system failed in the basement of Buidling 8 at the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. The primary asset damaged by the resulting flood was the NASA photo archive, located immediately below the location where the pipe failed. The photo archive is the property of NASA Headquarters and is administered by Goddard. It includes mostly duplicate negatives, transparencies, and photographs, but also does contain some original material. The investigation revealed that there was no document describing any agreement between HQ and Goddard concerning the conditions, duration, or expectations between the two parties regarding storage of the archives.

Lesson(s) Learned

A documented agreement is necessary even between NASA HQ and the field centers, or between field centers, when an asset owned by one organization is operated or stored by another organization. While a document would not have shielded the files from the deluge of water, it may have prevented the files from being in a vulnerable location in the first place or placed more stringent requirements on the storage conditions. It also would have enhanced the recovery efforts.

Recommendation(s)

Create a NASA HQ/Goddard agreement for the NASA photo archives that accomplishes the following:

  • Defines the nature of the collection
  • Defines the duration of NASA photo archive storage at Goddard
  • Identifies liabilities and responsibilities
  • Identifies emergency contacts
  • Identifies requisite environmental conditions for storage
  • Defines emergency film recovery procedures
