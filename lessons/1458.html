NASA Lesson #1458: Accident Investigations/Recorders

Lesson ID: #1458 (Database ID: 1458)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Columbia Accident Investigation Board (CAIB) experimented with different methods to record private witness interviews creating problems with transcriptions.

Lesson(s) Learned

For accident investigations, ground rules, processes and equipment necessary for review and revision of transcribed interviews should be established prior to the first interview.

Recommendation(s)

  • Assign a Support Staff member to pre-test recording equipment under a variety of circumstances (e.g. where interviews will actually take place) to make certain it will accomplish the task it is intended to accomplish.
  • Address room logistics, including room size, access control, and documentation control.
  • Use analog tape (not digital) for electronic recorders; it is easier to copy and duplicate, and the files are in a standard format unlike some digital devices. Laptop-based recording did not work for this investigation.
