NASA Lesson #0124: Payload Bay Duct Heater Overtemp

Lesson ID: #0124 (Database ID: 124)
Date: 1992-08-20
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The payload bay duct heater adds measured quantities of heat independently to each duct system. This raises the temperature of the process air from the cooling coils to the desired interface temperature leaving the portable purge unit and engine control system room. With no overtemp switches installed, excessive heat from the payload bay duct heaters burned through the duct insulation, causing severe damage with a potential for orbiter damage.

Lesson(s) Learned

Excessive heat from payload bay duct heaters may cause severe damage to ducting, insulation, and orbiter payload bay.

Recommendation(s)

Design and install high temperature cutoff switches in payload bay duct heaters for overtemp emergency shutdown.
