NASA Lesson #0985: Banana Plug Test Leads

Lesson ID: #0985 (Database ID: 985)
Date: 2001-08-01
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During Space Station hardware testing at Kennedy Space Center, a technician was in the process of connecting a current probe banana type plug test lead into a breakout box when he observed an arc. The arc caused the circuit protection to trip which safed the system. The exposed plug had contacted a grounding strap which caused the arc.

Lesson(s) Learned

The exposed banana plug was operating at 120v DC which can cause damage to equipment or injury to personnel.

Recommendation(s)

Modify existing banana plug leads in the inventory and to procure only new banana plug test leads that provide a deadfacing capability.
