NASA Lesson #0141: Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator Facility (RTGF) Lifting Operations

Lesson ID: #0141 (Database ID: 141)
Date: 1992-10-05
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The 10-ton crane at the RTGF is used to lift and position radioisotope thermoelectric generators and ground support equipment. Crane electrical component failure increases the risk of load and/or crane damage. Failure of switches 3 or 4 results in bridge movement and bridge brake will not engage if a BR-1 relay failure occurs. Failure of switches 5 or 6 results in trolley movement and trolley brake will not engage if a BR-2 relay failure occurs. Failure of switches 7 or 8 results in hoist movement and hoist brake will not engage if a BR-3 OR BR-4 relay failure occurs. Failure of switch SW-2 in the fast speed position will result in the hoist operating in the fast speed even if the operator selects slow speed.



Lesson(s) Learned

Failure of the RTGF crane electrical components, due to an internal malfunction, increases the potential for damage to load and/or crane.

Recommendation(s)

Install an emergency stop switch on the crane operator's control pendant to enable immediate power removal/setting of brakes in emergency situation.
