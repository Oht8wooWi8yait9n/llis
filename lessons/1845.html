Driving Event / Description

The Joint Base Operations and Support Contract (J-BOSC) was a joint initiative between NASA-KSC and the 45th Space Wing to combine base support requirements under a single contract for the furtherance of cost efficiencies, customer satisfaction and marketability of joint resources. The focus of the contract was to consolidate and streamline the processes and procedures customers use to identify, secure and account for services.

Lesson(s) Learned

Contracting

  • Not enough characteristics of the work to be performed were included to make the work statement clear. This made it difficult to manage contractor performance for specific elements of the statement of work. More detail should be included in all work statements.
  • The use of problem scenario's added no value to the evaluation process. Do not recommend their use.
  • The Source Evaluation Board (SEB) provided technical tasks descriptors to which the contractor responded to with a Statement of Work (SOW). These were insufficient in detail to include the government’s expectations. The SEB should have been the entity to develop and determined what was included within the Statement of Work.
  • The contract should ensure compatibility of systems and demonstrate (not assume) the capabilities. SEB personnel should ensure proposed system is compatible with other Management Information Systems (MIS) in use. Contractors need to upgrade systems equal to the current level used by the government.

Administration

  • The contractor may perceive some documents generated in support of the contract as proprietary, especially in the cost and budgeting area. Some documents generated by the contractor were not provided to the government. Include in the contract that all documents generated in support of the contract will be made available to the government upon request.
  • Do not allow for complicated development and formatting of sections of the contact for they result in making it difficult to modify.
  • Use of electronic submission of proposals with a specific format identified enhanced the review process.
  • Metrics should be established as part of the initial contract to include the areas to be measured, the source of the data, what the data represents and where the data is maintained and frequency it will be provided. Incorporate requirements into the initial contract & determine which areas to measure up front.
  • Include in the contract that all documents generated in support of the contract will be made available to the government upon request.

Recommendation(s)

  1. More detail should be included in all work statements.
  2. Metrics should be established as part of the initial contract up front.
  3. Ensure proposed Information Systems are compatible with other MIS in use.
