NASA Lesson #1424: Marketing Project Contributions, Successes and Research Results to Customers and Management (Knowledge Management)

Lesson ID: #1424 (Database ID: 1424)
Date: 2004-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Despite the importance of the Project's research results to its customers and stakeholders, it had little visibility and advocacy within the NASA community. This environment may have contributed to the cancellation of the project.

Key Words/Phrases: Stake holders, Visibility, Advocacy

Lesson(s) Learned

Lack of project promotion to upper management and inadequate or slow communication of project results can result in an inaccurate appraisal of a project's value to its customers. High visibility and timely communication increase a project's opportunities for recognition and gaining/maintaining support. Low visibility and slow communication may increase a project's vulnerability to budget cuts, milestone de-scoping and a perception that the research results are unimportant or too incremental to merit continued support.

Recommendation(s)

In order to enhance project support, success, and advocacy, each program should utilize methods and tools that create a teamwork environment, open communication, and ownership of tasks. To ensure the Project's merits are recognized, in addition to standard reviews and presentations, Project management should develop a web site that serves as a project archive and showcase that would be used to integrate and disseminate the needed information effectively, conveniently, and compatibly with the audience's requirements.
