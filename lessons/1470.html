NASA Lesson #1470: Accident Investigations/Document Management, Retention, and Archival Policy for an Independent Investigation

Lesson ID: #1470 (Database ID: 1470)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Columbia Accident Investigation Board (CAIB) investigators were initially reluctant to submit documents to the document management staff (Department of Justice and their contract, Aspen Systems)) for inclusion to the CAIB Database Server; they expressed a desire to receive guidelines and direction directly from the Board before providing sensitive documents and work product.

Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Early in the investigation, the Board did not adopt a document management and archival policy. Consequently, it was difficult for document management staff to obtain CAIB generated documents from the investigators and members of the Board.

  3. Investigators considered document management, archiving, and designating documents for release to the public as analogous processes. This made it considerably difficult for the document management staff to obtain documents from investigators for inclusion into the CAIB Database Server.

Recommendation(s)

  • Have the General Counsel and/or document management support staff establish written guidelines for document management, retention, and archiving at the beginning of an independent investigation. Present these guidelines to the investigation board for approval and then circulate them to the investigators and support staff for action.
  • Place document management support staff within each investigative group at the beginning of the investigation. The representatives may be able to assist the investigators in understanding and adhering to the document management, retention, and archiving policies, thus reducing concerns about sensitive documents becoming part of the public record.
