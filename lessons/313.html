NASA Lesson #0313: Electrical Cable, Splicing

Lesson ID: #0313 (Database ID: 313)
Date: 1993-08-03
Organization: Marshall Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The shuttle IUS was not designed to be maintained. Consequently, no electrical connectors were used. During system testing, some components failed. In the process of replacing the failed components, electrical reconnection was made by splicing. Cables that were respliced were shorter in length and less reliable.

Lesson(s) Learned

Splicing of electrical cables reduces the length and may increase the probability of cable malfunction.

Recommendation(s)

In designs where cutting of electrical cables and subsequent reconnect by splicing may become necessary, provide sufficient slack in the cable to allow splicing.

Evidence / Additional Context

It can be seen by review of design that provision of slack would decrease the likelihood of placing additional strain on a spliced wire, and consequently decrease the probability of cable failure.
