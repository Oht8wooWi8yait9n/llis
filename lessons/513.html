NASA Lesson #0513: Moisture Contamination from the Initial Electronically Scanned Pressure (ESP) Systems Module Destroyed all Remaining ESP Modules

Lesson ID: #0513 (Database ID: 513)
Date: 1994-07-29
Organization: Langley Research Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Lesson(s) Learned

The reference cavities for all ESP modules are connected to a common vacuum source; if this common vacuum source becomes contaminated with moisture from the initial ESP module all remaining ESP modules can be destroyed.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Ensure that high pressure tubes are correctly connected to the proper high pressure module ports by maintaining thorough tube labeling and verifying that tubes are labeled correctly after every instrumentation change.
  2. Examine tubing paths between measurement orifices and sensor ports, looking for potential interfaces with fluids other than dry, clean, inert gas.
  3. Always leak check sensors with vacuum only.
