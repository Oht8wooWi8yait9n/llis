NASA Lesson #0900: Working with Foreign Contributors to NASA Missions/Flight Assurance

Lesson ID: #0900 (Database ID: 900)
Date: 2000-07-31
Organization: Goddard Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Schedules that had been agreed, on closer examination, turned out to mean something different than parties expected; design loads presented to NASA for use in designing a mission payload were wildly conservative & could lead to significant cost increases.

Lesson(s) Learned

In PI-mode missions with a foreign partner making a major hardware contribution, reconciliation during the proposal stage of NASA's and the partner's approaches to setting environmental standards and structuring environmental tests and QA program features may avoid protracted delays later. A good place to start is with definition of terms, e.g., Preliminary Design Review may not mean the same thing to CNES as it does to NASA.

Recommendation(s)

Early agreement on definition of terms & on environmental standards, test, and QA programs; need to avoid policy of not insisting on an appropriate level of insight just because foreign partner has offered a contribution; could be a significant risk to NASA's investment in the mission.
