NASA Lesson #4036: Refractory Removal

Lesson ID: #4036 (Database ID: 4036)
Date: 2010-08-12
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Implementation
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Facilities, Ground processing and manifesting, Ground support systems, Maintenance
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Each Space Shuttle launch erodes or damages the refractory cement of the MFD. For many years, the damaged cement was removed by hand with pneumatic tools. This method was dangerous and time-consuming, and it caused significant damage to the underlying reinforcement steel. The reinforcement steel had to be repaired or replaced after each removal process.

Lesson(s) Learned

A method of using ultra-high-pressure water jetting was introduced, enabling the refractory materials to be removed faster, in a safer manner, and without damage to the underlying reinforcement steel. This process allowed repairs to be made in less time without disrupting launch schedules.



Figure 1: A mechanic using a high pressure wand

Recommendation(s)

Use ultra-high-pressure water jetting in all future projects that require the removal of concrete.
