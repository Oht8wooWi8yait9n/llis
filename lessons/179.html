NASA Lesson #0179: Solid Rocket Motor (SRM) Static Testing

Lesson ID: #0179 (Database ID: 179)
Date: 1992-10-23
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

A review of the considerations for the decision to fire the full scale motors in the horizontal position was based mostly on programmatic considerations and the concern of determining the thrust values more accurately. It was not obvious that the effects of this deviation in test and flight configuration received sufficient attention.



Lesson(s) Learned

The full scale hot fire testing of the SRM's was performed with the motors in the horizontal position rather than in flight attitude.

Recommendation(s)

Perform hot fire static testing in a vertical (flight attitude) position unless a deviation will produce proven sufficient results.
