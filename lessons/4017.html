NASA Lesson #4017: Time and Material Task Order Contracts

Lesson ID: #4017 (Database ID: 4017)
Date: 2010-08-13
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Implementation
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Facilities, Acquisition / procurement strategy and planning, Risk management, Maintenance
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Fixed-price contracts (FPCs) have been and remain the preferred method of procuring outside contractor support for NASA at KSC. A very large FPC contract was awarded to perform corrosion control on launch pad 39A. While performing the corrosion control efforts on the pad structure, it was discovered that much of the corrosion damage requiring repair was hidden and could not be identified until work was underway. This resulted in many change orders. Issuing change orders resulted in significant cost growth and schedule slip.

Lesson(s) Learned

An FPC is not the most efficient way to perform corrosion-control-type work. Using a TMTO contract enables better cost control and gives NASA more flexibility to control the contract work and to maintain a schedule.

Recommendation(s)

TMTO contracting should be considered when a scope of work cannot be clearly identified or sufficient engineering data is not available.
