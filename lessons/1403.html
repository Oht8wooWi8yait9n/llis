NASA Lesson #1403: Improvement of Agency-Wide Support Functions are Required to Implement the One NASA Organization

Lesson ID: #1403 (Database ID: 1403)
Date: 2004-05-09
Organization: Langley Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The requirement for the NESC originated as recommendation R7.5-1 from the Columbia Accident Investigation Board (CAIB), (CAIB Report, Volume I, pg. 193). To be effective, the NESC was charted as a One NASA organization. Organizing under the One NASA model proved difficult, due in part to unexpected issues with Agency-wide support functions. The inconsistency continually impeded progress toward the goal of a full operating capability and caused undue stress to personnel.

Lesson(s) Learned

The NESC is located at LaRC. A portion of the NESC work force is located at LaRC while other NESC employees are located at their home Centers and report to their NESC supervisors at LaRC. The following issues occurred for NESC employees remaining at their home centers:

  • Medical Records
    • Employee medical records were transferred to storage or forwarded to LaRC instead of staying at the home Center clinic where the employee is assigned
  • Directory Services
    • The employee's listings in the X.500 Directory Service were deleted
    • The employee's One NASA email address was deleted
  • Payroll
    • Combined Federal Campaign (CFC) payroll deductions stopped
    • Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) deductions stopped
    • NASA Employee Benefits (NEBA) deductions stopped
    • Access to Employee Express blocked
    • Leave balances did not transfer
    • Allotments stopped
  • NASA Training
    • SOLAR account shut down
  • Financial Disclosure Form 450
    • Home Center Chief Counsel refused to answer employee questions about Form 450 and referred them to LaRC Chief Counsel
  • IT
    • Virtual Private Network users cannot access all required resources behind LaRC firewall
  • Travel Manager
    • All NESC employees ultimately required to have LaRC Travel Manager Account since NESC supervisors at LaRC could not approve travel entered into another Center's Travel Manager system.
    • NESC Chief Engineers at home Centers could not approve travel for non-NESC employees because they cannot have supervisor privileges in their home Center's Travel Manager system. NASA employees outside of the NESC are frequently used on NESC projects.
  • Property Control
    • Property used by a NESC employee at a home Center stayed under home Center Property Control System. Should be tagged to the NESC. When Property Loan Agreements are required, there is no NESC Supervisor at the respective Centers (all NESC Supervisors reside at LaRC). All future NESC funded equipment will be tagged to LaRC.
  • Badge Requirements/Access to Buildings
    • Some NESC employees were unable to enter facilities at their home Center due to being hard-lined to LaRC even though they kept their badge from the home Center.
  • Security
    • Once NESC employees are reassigned to the NESC, their home Center's Office of Security has no record these employees are still civil service employees and are on the Agency's roles.

Recommendation(s)

For One NASA organizations to operate more efficiently and effectively, improvements to Agency's support functions should continue. Suggest the Agency process “mock” personnel actions that reassign civil service employees across the Agency and identify which processes and procedures need to be worked. This will allow the Agency to develop real-time solutions that will limit similar problems in the future. One process is being developed for Property Control issues where all NESC equipment will have LaRC property tags, regardless of where (which Center) the property resides.
