NASA Lesson #1477: Accident Investigations/Minutes Taking Support to Board

Lesson ID: #1477 (Database ID: 1477)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Columbia Task Force (CTF) was tasked with hiring minutes-taking support for the Columbia Accident Investigation Board (CAIB). The CTF hired safety and mission assurance/engineering personnel from The Aerospace Corporation to do this task. These personnel were not skilled in minutes taking and regressed to a process of audio recording of board deliberations. The Aerospace personnel then provided quasi-transcripts of these tapes as minutes. The products were not delivered in a timely fashion (sometimes weeks after the meeting) for review by the CAIB executive secretary and were, for the most part, of very poor quality. Tracking of meeting minutes was also problematic with the Aerospace personnel. This problem was remedied finally by the hiring of a skilled minutes-taking person who produced a quality set of minutes within hours of the board meeting.

Lesson(s) Learned

To support the minutes taking requirements of an accident investigation board in a professional manner, the hiring of non-skilled personnel to do this task is totally unacceptable.

Recommendation(s)

  • Hire professional meeting minute takers at the outset of the investigation
  • Add this requirement to the Contingency Action Plan (CAP)
