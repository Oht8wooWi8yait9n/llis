NASA Lesson #0860: Facilities, Mod & Rehab, Added Team Member

Lesson ID: #0860 (Database ID: 860)
Date: 1999-08-23
Organization: Glenn Research Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Mod and Rehab projects are typically requiring that people be moved out of offices while the construction activity is taking place. As a result, there is an extra burden on the Project Manager to coordinate people moves. Along with the moves come the issues of telephone and computer moves, because different organizations handle these moves. In addition, there is also the issue of where are the people going to be temporarily moved to.

During the planning phases of moves, it becomes a full-time job to coordinate all the above activities.

Lesson(s) Learned

It becomes a logistics nightmare for a Project Manager to coordinate people moves in a Mod and Rehab project.

Recommendation(s)

Include in the Project Team a Move Coordinator. This person would work along with the Project Manager to ensure a smooth transition for the inconvenienced building occupants.
