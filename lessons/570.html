NASA Lesson #0570: SCUBA hose failure

Lesson ID: #0570 (Database ID: 570)
Date: 1997-09-23
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The low pressure buoyancy compensator flex hose failed when it was inadvertently attached to the high pressure port of the SCUBA regulator. When the diver opened the SCUBA bottle for a pre dive check the hose failed causing minor injuries to the diver.

Lesson(s) Learned

Color coding of similar connections is an effective method to ensure the correct assembly prior to use.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Color coding of similar connections is an effective method to ensure the correct assembly of equipment prior to use.
  2. Use only SCUBA regulators with different size fitting for high and low pressure outlets. Color code high and low pressure hoses for identification. Test to verify correct assembly.
