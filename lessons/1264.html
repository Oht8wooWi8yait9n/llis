NASA Lesson #1264: A Thorough Understanding of Model Mount (sting, arc sector, etc) Design Loads is Required by All Key Test Personnel

Lesson ID: #1264 (Database ID: 1264)
Date: 2002-07-01
Organization: Langley Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

While testing at Mach 0.8 in the 16-ft Transonic Tunnel, a full-scale F-15 inlet model separated from the sting. The model traveled downstream and impacted the catch screen and turning vanes. The impact resulted in total loss of the model and damage to the screen. Some debris got through the screen and damaged the wind tunnel blades. The sting was also damaged.

An investigation team concluded that the aerodynamic loads on the model resulted in sting loads that exceeded the ultimate strength of the sting. Possible reasons the large loads occurred are: operating at a Mach Number not in the original test envelope, F-15 cowl traveled past intended angle, sting deflection, and dynamic loads.

THERE ARE 6 LESSONS IN LLIS FOR THIS DRIVING EVENT.

Lesson(s) Learned

During the course of the investigation, the mishap board concluded that several key personnel did not have a thorough understanding of the model mount (sting, arc sector, etc) load limits. The personnel only knew the values, not the details behind them. This lack of understanding did not contribute to this mishap, but it could contribute to future mishaps if not addressed.

Recommendation(s)

A thorough explanation of model mount (sting, arc sector, etc) load limits should be available and made to all facility personnel.

Evidence / Additional Context

Pretest and pre-run meetings are now in place along with a check list that properly identifies and documents worst case test conditions and limits. Worst-case test conditions and failure mode analysis are now included as part of a new branch position "AST Facility Systems Safety Engineer". This function specifically looks at test as a system to identify failure modes or undesired events. Based on the failure scenarios, additional countermeasures or state-of-the-art analysis is performed prior to the Facility Safety Head approving test runs to begin. A thorough explanation of model mount (sting, arc sector, etc) load limits is explained and made available to all facility personnel. At the pretest meeting the Facility Safety Head identifies most highly loaded component in the sting and describes what condition causes the load.
