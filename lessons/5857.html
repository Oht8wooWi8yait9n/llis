NASA Lesson #5857: Improved Scope Description between NASA programs and projects

Lesson ID: #5857 (Database ID: 5857)
Date: 2011-06-17
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: Formulation
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Topic / Category: Program planning / development and management
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The event driving this lesson learned is the pilot conducted on the Constellation Program Extravehicular Activity (EVA) Project as part of the Agency’s Earned Value Management (EVM) Capability Project.

Lesson(s) Learned

Programs and projects need documented agreements of requirements and expectations of expected results/products/scope within an agreed upon budget and schedule. These scope descriptions need to be well defined to ensure understanding of requirements.

Recommendation(s)

Develop more of a customer/supplier relationship between NASA programs/projects. Develop a scope description/statement of work between the program and the project to provide a basis for in-scope vs. out-of-scope discussions.

Evidence / Additional Context

Documented agreements of scope, schedule, and budget between NASA programs and projects. Scope descriptions that provide shall statements and sufficient high level requirements to allow projects to develop a reasonable Performance Measurement Baseline (PMB) and appropriate Management Reserve (MR).
