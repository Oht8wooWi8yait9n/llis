NASA Lesson #1356: ADEOS-II NGN L0 Data Gap Introduction Problem

Lesson ID: #1356 (Database ID: 1356)
Date: 2003-07-16
Organization: Goddard Space Flight Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

In early February 2003, JPL and NASDA reported gaps in Level 0 data products received from the Wallops and Alaska ADEOS-II Data Processing Systems. Gaps in the SeaWinds product were characterized by JPL to be intermittent in nature, of variable size, and located at irregular intervals in the data products. Reprocessing of the raw data by NASDA yielded 100% data recovery. Thus, while there was 100% data capture, data gaps were introduced by the DS resulting in approximately 8% data loss in the Level 0 product (i.e. 8 out of every 100 minutes of data was not included in the Level 0 product). These gaps were undetected at the processing station until reported by JPL and NASDA.

Lesson(s) Learned

Assess full budget impacts of launch delays and plan for technology refresh

Recommendation(s)

Complete cost analysis should look beyond continuing current efforts and capabilities to identify new needs that might result from the launch delay. One such example is technology refresh. Another example might include the need to repeat training as the time between training and operations extends, the liklihood of personnel loss increases. When significant launch delays occur either individually or as a result of a series of delays, conduct sustaining engineering analysis to verify that equipment aging has not become an issue. Contingency funding should be included in budgets to offset this issue.
