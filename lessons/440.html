NASA Lesson #0440: Radio Frequency Assignments (~1976)

Lesson ID: #0440 (Database ID: 440)
Date: 1996-07-19
Organization: Jet Propulsion Laboratory
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

(Relevant Historical Lesson(s) Learned)

The Office of Telecommunications Policy (OTP) in cooperation with the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) has established a procedure for the review of telecommunications systems for frequency availability and electromagnetic compatibility. Late transfer of SEASAT-A project responsibility to JPL resulted in delayed preparation and submission of the requisite review material.

The choice of the Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) frequency especially was questionable for a few months. Frequency management personnel from several government agencies performed in "Tiger Team" fashion to coordinate authorization of a specific frequency.

Direct project impact was the delay in knowing this important parameter and the concomitant delay in the detailed design of the SAR.

Additional Keyword(s): Conceptual Planning, RF Assignments

Lesson(s) Learned

Preparation and submission of radio frequency review material should be accomplished in the conceptual stage of a project.

Recommendation(s)

Preparation and submission of radio frequency review material should be accomplished in the conceptual stage of a project.

Evidence / Additional Context


JPL has referenced this lesson learned as additional rationale and guidance supporting Paragraph 5.2 ("Management Practices: Planning") in the Jet Propulsion Laboratory standard "Flight Project Practices, Rev. 7," JPL DocID 58032, September 30, 2008.
