NASA Lesson #6977: Tools Used During Program and Project Life Cycle Reviews

Lesson ID: #6977 (Database ID: 6977)
Date: 2013-03-12
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Approval
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Program level review processes
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During the GSDO SRR/SDR a review portal and a comment collection tool were used. A survey of review participants was conducted after the close of the review. The survey identified some positive findings and areas for improvement of future reviews.

Lesson(s) Learned

  • Positive finding: Having a centralized portal for all materials related to the lifecycle review is important. This portal should be opened prior to review kickoff.
    • Area for improvement: the review portal needs to be updated frequently during the course of the review with meeting minutes and meeting agendas (especially for lower level panels).
    • Area for improvement: The review portal should have a document search capability to make it easy to locate products to be reviewed.

  • Positive finding: A tool for collecting, updating and dispositioning comments was used for the GSDO SRR/SDR. It is important that this tool be very easy and intuitive for all commenters, comment sponsors and administrators.
    • Area for improvement: It is important that comment status and disposition be immediately updated and available at the review portal. Once a Board or Panel makes decisions the comment tool should be updated the same day if possible. A very useful function for this tool to have is the ability to sort comments by disposition (accepted, withdrawn, etc.) as well as comment status (sponsored, not sponsored etc.).

Recommendation(s)

Programs or Projects conducting Life Cycle Reviews should consider both the positive findings and areas for improvement identified above when designing their review portal and providing updates to their review information.
