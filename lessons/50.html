NASA Lesson #0050: Delta Wiring Harness Connectors and Installation

Lesson ID: #0050 (Database ID: 50)
Date: 1991-12-24
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Premature shutdown of the main and vernier engines occurred as a result of an electrical malfunction in the vehicle's power bus, specifically a short circuit to ground. A Mishap Board investigation concluded the most probable cause of the failure was flight vibration that induced mechanical damage to wiring insulation resulting in a short to ground.

Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Wiring harness connectors of critical components must be capable of withstanding vibration induced stresses.
  2. Failure to protect wiring harnesses from abrasive surfaces and sharp edges of structural members will lead to wire damage caused by flight vibration.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Identify and implement improvements in the quality review, inspection, and testing of connectors in proportion to their criticality.
  2. Prior to future launches, perform megger checks of all critical wiring harnesses and inspect harnesses to verify quality of installation.
