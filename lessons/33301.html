NASA Lesson #33301: Chilled Water Line Failure Under Facility Concrete Slab Resulted In Over $1.5M In Facility Damages

Lesson ID: #33301 (Database ID: 33301)
Date: 2022-12-22
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Topic / Category: Engineering Design, Safety and Mission Assurance, Accident Investigation, Facilities, Acquisition / procurement strategy and planning, Early requirements and standards definition, Maintenance, Engineering design and project processes and standards
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Kennedy Space Center ​Operations Support Building (OSB) 1 experienced a catastrophic failure of the PVC chilled water return line which ruptured the concrete floors in the southwest corner of the building and flooded a portion of the first floor. The chilled water campus loop had been leaking for years and multiple attempts to find the source of the leak were not successful, because the source of the leak was an elbow under the facility. Personnel in the damaged area of the facility evacuated. The catastrophic failure resulted in a chilled water plant shutdown due to water loss in the system. This resulted in a loss of cooling to all the facilities that are supplied by the chilled water plant including the Launch Control Center and other essential buildings.  A Mishap Investigation Team documented the findings in NMIS Case Number: 22-101302.

Lesson(s) Learned

Chilled water pipe should not be routed under facilities because it can't be inspected, and long term, is subject to stresses induced from settling foundations and shifting soils.

Recommendation(s)

PVC should not be used for chilled water piping either outside, inside or under facilities.

  1. Do not allow the use of PVC piping for chilled water lines inside or outside facilities. 
  2. Do not allow chilled water piping to be routed under floors/concrete inside facilities.

SpecsIntact Master specification 33 61 14 section 2.2.3 and 2.2.4 currently allows for Plastic Reinforced Thermosetting Resin (RTR) and PVC piping in chilled water systems outside facilities.  SpecsIntact Master specification 23 64 26 section 1.7.3 Accessibility requires pipes be accessible inside facilities. A clarifying statement is chilled water pipe should not be routed under facilities because it can't be inspected, and long term, is subject to stresses induced from settling foundations and shifting soils.

Evidence / Additional Context

The KSC Construction of Facilities Engineering section has updated the Statement of Work (SOW) template to preclude the use of PVC piping in chilled water systems, and to disallow the routing under facility slabs. This template is used when new facility projects are being developed.
