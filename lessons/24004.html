NASA Lesson #24004: Closed-Loop Tracking of Life Cycle Review Inputs

Lesson ID: #24004 (Database ID: 24004)
Date: 2018-02-01
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Topic / Category: Program Management, Configuration and data management, Review systems and boards
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The EGS Program concluded its CDR late in 2015. During subsequent briefings to the NASA Directorate Program Management Council (DPMC) and Agency Program Management Council (APMC) discussions the EGS Program’s Standing Review Board (SRB) submitted RFAs. The RFAs were worked and closed with the appropriate Agency council. EGS did not close-loop track all RID closures and maintain closure evidence along with the RFAs CDR.  Some of the RIDs, RFAs and action items could not be closed until the EGS Program could address the issues with all stakeholders and this coordination took time.

When preparing for the Systems Integration Review (SIR), EGS prepared status information and closure evidence for all RIDs, RFAs and action items. It took significant resources to pull this data together because the inputs were closed at a number of different boards, some of the action items had actually been closed during the CDR Board, some at program or cross-program Boards and other forums.

Lesson(s) Learned

Programs and projects should track all RIDs, RFAs and Action Items along with closure evidence throughout the entire LCR and all associated follow-on discussions or out-briefs. These discussions or out-briefs could be to any entity “above” the program or project such as the Center, DPMC or APMC.

There should be one program POC to oversee the entire collection of all RIDs, RFAs and Action Items. The POC should provide a single place to collect all closure evidence as received. The employee to whom the item was assigned should be given the responsibility and held accountable to ensure the item is closed and that closure evidence is provided to the POC and stored in the single location.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Programs or projects should establish a single process for documenting, tracking and ensuring closure of all RIDs, RFAs and Action Items related to a LCR no matter when or where the items come from.  
  2. Hold each assignee accountable for closing out items they are assigned and providing closure evidence in the single designated location and ensure management is aware of closure status metrics.
  3. Designate a specific person responsible for holding assignees accountable, ensuring closure of and tracking closure evidence for all RIDs, RFAs and Action Items associated with every part of the end-to-end activities of LCRs and follow-on discussions with Decision Authorities and other stakeholders.
