NASA Lesson #0095: OPF Extendable Platforms

Lesson ID: #0095 (Database ID: 95)
Date: 1992-07-31
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Technicians were in the process of extending platform 10-2 in OPF HB-1. After extending the platform about 3 feet, the observer noted that the platform would not clear the body flap and signaled a "halt." Before the platform could be stopped, it contacted two tiles, damaging them.

Lesson(s) Learned

Failure to measure and verify adequate clearance, prior to platform extension could result in damage to orbiter thermal protection tiles.

Recommendation(s)

Review platform extension procedures to ensure verification of ample clearance prior to extending platforms.
