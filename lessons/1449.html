NASA Lesson #1449: Accident Investigations/Action Request System

Lesson ID: #1449 (Database ID: 1449)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

For the Columbia accident investigation, the Action/Request for Information Form format took a long time to establish, which caused many investigators to use obsolete and discontinued formats. A significant amount of time was spent backtracking and cataloging actions and responses/data that were created prior to selecting a specific format.

Lesson(s) Learned

Complex forms will take endless iterations to implement evenly across all organizations and may cause the action authorization process to break down.

Recommendation(s)

  • Establish the Action Request process within the first days of Board arrival.
  • Have all action/information requests screened by Task Force technical leads to ensure the requests are detailed enough to be accurately interpreted during initial review by NASA data providers and specifically germane to the investigation. Forms, tracking logs, and files/database must accommodate sensitive/proprietary (legal, medical, contractual, national security, export control (ITAR)) data and generic/public data required by the Investigative Board as well as unsolicited data provided by the public, private industry, and/or political sector.
  • Establish an authoritative lead immediately for sensitive/secure data to determine what data should be evaluated and marked (export controlled, sensitive, proprietary, etc.) or whether all documentation should be protected with a global/generic statement.
  • Inform the Board early on known sensitive data process requirements. Ensure that this authority reviews any data that are to be released to the public.
  • Audit the system process regularly.

Board and TF CM leads must:

  • Create an action tracking form immediately that captures actions and closure response processes tailored to the accident specifics (recommend the attached).
