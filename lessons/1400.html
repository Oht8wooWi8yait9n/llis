NASA Lesson #1400: Resources Management: Resource Loading

Lesson ID: #1400 (Database ID: 1400)
Date: 2003-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Project, like many others, was susceptible to the changing requirements and priorities characteristic of research and development activities. Since the Project was one of several in its parent Program's portfolio, additional challenges arose in times of Agency resource adjustment. Although, the Project was successful at meeting its objectives, the Program often elected to increase the funding of its other projects (projects which were not as successful in meeting their milestones but may have been more successful at advocating for additional resources). The result was that the Project's efficiency and effectiveness, while high, was at times hindered because it could not secure needed resources during its critical phases.

Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Success at meeting a project's milestones may not translate into a stable allotment of resources.

  3. Those projects not meeting all their requirements may still get rewarded with increased resources if their needs are communicated effectively to their funding sources.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Projects must place effort on identifying risks associated with resource allocations. The project must have a plan that describes when and how funding sources and other stakeholders learn about potential drawbacks of resource cuts.

  3. Projects should include, in addition to their risk planning, planning for opportunities (a scope increasing strategy). These opportunities once identified and mapped into the project's goals and objectives should be used, in addition to increasing the Project's payoff, as mechanisms to secure, and at times increase, a project's resources.
