NASA Lesson #3641: Replacement of the Automated Wire Analyzer (AWA)

Lesson ID: #3641 (Database ID: 3641)
Date: 2010-08-30
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Implementation
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Ground Equipment, Ground processing and manifesting, Maintenance
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The AWA would not operate during a validation test on an SMCH. The only technician available to repair the AWA had to be brought in from out of the area. The cost of parts and the downtime required for repair were not acceptable for continued use of the AWA. Because each SMCH must be validated before being installed in the Orbiter, any interruption in harness testing creates delays in all subsequent tasks in the Orbiter flow process.

Lesson(s) Learned

The continually decreasing cost of off-the-shelf test equipment versus the cost and time necessary to maintain old equipment and the risk of unreliable data makes it cost-effective to continually look at using evolving test equipment available on the market.

Recommendation(s)

Continue to evaluate the benefits and the cost of replacing outdated equipment by checking the market for innovative replacements.

Evidence / Additional Context

There have been no additional costs since the new system has been installed, and the time necessary to validate the SMCHs has been reduced by approximately 90 percent.
