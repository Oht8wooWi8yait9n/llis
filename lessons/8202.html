Driving Event / Description

The Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) spacecraft is a complex system comprised of a Rover, Descent Stage (DS), Cruise Stage, Backshell, and Heatshield. The DS (Figure 1) is a large structure that incorporates the Sky Crane function and includes the electric, data and propulsion systems necessary to control the MSL descent to the Martian surface.
