NASA Lesson #1848: Lessons Learned from the Administration of the Joint Base Operations Support Contract (J-BOSC) at KSC

Lesson ID: #1848 (Database ID: 1848)
Date: 2008-04-16
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Topic / Category: Administration / Organization, Communication Systems, Business processes, Communications between different offices and contractor personnel
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Cape Canaveral Spaceport Management Office (CCMO ) performed management insight for the activities of the Joint Base Operations and Support Contract (J-BOSC), a joint initiative between NASA-KSC and the 45th Space Wing to combine base support requirements under a single contract. CCSMO was responsible for administering the J-BOSC. CCSMO was jointly managed by both agencies for the furtherance of cost efficiencies, customer satisfaction and marketability of joint resources. The focus of the contract was to consolidate and streamline the processes and procedures customers use to identify, secure and account for services. CCSMO led the partnering activities necessary between state, local and commercial enterprises to further the vision of a national spaceport.

Lesson(s) Learned

Administrative
  • The CCMO administrative office was not fully recognized as a 45th SW Staff organization and was often left out of the loop for actions and opportunities.
  • Lack of clerical support. Many CCMO employees were tasked to keep official files, a job many have not been properly trained in. Such a critical responsibility should not be distributed and left to individual interpretation. Since the only clerical support was at the executive level, support to the rest of the organization was not well defined or understood.
Award Fee
  • Absence of an approved surveillance plan prior to contract start caused much confusion during the first award fee period. The “5 objective” approach to surveillance used during the 1st period had to be modified later to conform to an Areas of Emphasis letter.
  • Schedule: The Integrated Product Teams (IPTs) and Board of Director review added complexity to the process of providing an award fee determination within 45 days after the end of the period.
  • Customers’ direct input along with the IPT process required rework on the part of IPT leads to validate and resolve input to ensure it does not conflict with the evaluation results of the IPT. all customers to use the IPT forum as their conduit (as the IPTs were intended), rather than sending it directly.
Communication Systems The greatest challenge facing effectiveness was the communication systems (both LAN and phone). Countless complaints from outside customers confused about how to leave messages on the NASA answering system were received. The firewall issue prevented us from accessing software programs, websites and network servers that are crucial to our day to day responsibilities.

Recommendation(s)

  • Critical responsibility to keep official files should not be distributed and left to individual interpretation. Clerical support should be provided.
  • Customer input processes (such as the IPT forums) should be established and maintained
  • An organization interested in pursuing a Joint Venture should get the communication system figured out first.
