Driving Event / Description

The FASTSAT was developed at an unprecedented speed and low cost, while fulfilling the primary objectives of providing low Earth orbit opportunities for satellite experiments. It was developed to demonstrate the satellite bus and mission integration approach and to demonstrate Poly-Pico Orbital Deployer (PPOD) launch capability of a nanosat class spacecraft from the satellite bus. The unique development approach of the FASTSAT yielded 24 lessons learned in the areas of Program Management, Engineering, Systems Engineering, and Safety and Mission Assurance.

Lesson(s) Learned

This lessons learned report captures findings that will benefit future FASTSAT satellites and other projects interested in pushing the boundaries for cost and schedule. These findings include both best practices that were found to be helpful, and issues that should be avoided on future projects.

Recommendation(s)

The FASTSAT Lessons Learned Report contains some lessons learned that are recommendations to repeat what worked well for the project, and some are recommendations for changes in the future related to Project Management, Engineering, Systems Engineering, and Safety and Mission Assurance.
