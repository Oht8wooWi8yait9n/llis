NASA Lesson #1453: Accident Investigations/Office Equipment

Lesson ID: #1453 (Database ID: 1453)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

For the Columbia accident investigation, hastily arranged contract for copier support and other devices caused contractual nightmares.

Lesson(s) Learned

Do not wait for the accident investigation board or Task Force to ask for standard office electronics (B&W/Color printers and copiers, fax machines, TV's with VCR and DVD/CD, CD Burners, etc.).

Recommendation(s)

  • Make accident investigation equipment/sources for equipment available for immediate installation the day work begins.
  • Add creature comfort items if it is apparent that the investigation will be lengthy (e.g., refrigerators, microwaves, etc.)
