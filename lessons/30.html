NASA Lesson #0030: Gaseous Nitrogen (GN2) Tank Lifting Operations

Lesson ID: #0030 (Database ID: 30)
Date: 1992-09-11
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During installation of the orbiter GN2 tanks, the H70-0649 lifting adapter is used to raise the GN2 tank from the orbiter processing facility floor to the orbiter payload bay. The adapter is designed for the tank to sit flush in the cradle, but its configuration allows only the GN2 tank boss threads to sit on the adapter cradle. As a result, the tank boss supports the total tank load. This "improper fit" condition contributes to the inability to properly secure the GN2 tank during lifting operations, possibly resulting in damage to the tank, the orbiter payload bay, and/or personnel injury.



Lesson(s) Learned

  1. The lifting adapter prohibits tank from sitting flush in cradle due to a 1.8" tank boss extension, allowing boss threads to support total load.
  2. The strap securing the GN2 tank to the lifting adapter provides an inadequate securing capability during lifting operations.


Recommendation(s)

  1. Redesign/modify lifting adapter and cradle to relieve boss from supporting the total GN2 tank load during lifting operations.
  2. Design a more adjustable strap that will more effectively tighten around the tank for a more secure fit.
