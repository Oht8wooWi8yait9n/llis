NASA Lesson #21803: Employee Falls Down Steep Ramp at Door without a Landing

Lesson ID: #21803 (Database ID: 21803)
Date: 2015-10-05
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: Not Applicable
Topic / Category: Accident Investigation, Facilities, Mishap Reporting, Safety & Mission Assurance, Maintenance
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

An employee attempted to enter an older facility using an entrance that has a steep ramp without a landing at the doorway. After ascending the ramp the employee grasped the door handle and pulled. Unfortunately, the door flexed and rebounded causing the employee to lose balance and fall down the ramp. Upon falling, the employee’s head struck the pavement of the adjacent parking lot and suffered a permanent-partial injury.

Upon investigation it was found that the ramp leading into the facility had a slope of 1:6.27 (9 feet 5 inches run with a drop of 1 feet 6 inches) which exceeds the California Building Code for Historical Buildings (Accessibility Section, 8-603.6). In addition, it was found that the egress/ingress way had no handrails and it and the grounds outside the building were covered with leaves which impaired footing and visibility.

 

Figure 1: Photo of doorway after mitigations

Lesson(s) Learned

Egress/ingress ramps without doorway landings and handrails should not be used.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Supervisors and Facility Managers should ensure that employees have a safe means of building egress/ingress by conducting periodic inspections and closing off passages that are unsafe until corrective actions are implemented.
  2. All employees should refrain from using unsafe building egress/ingress passages and report them to their supervisor and/or facility manager.

Evidence / Additional Context

The ramp was measured to be 9 feet 5 inches run with a drop of 1 feet 6 inches. This translates to 1:6.27 which exceeds the California Building Code for Historical Buildings (Accessibility Section, 8-603.6)

Photographs showing that the ramp lacks a handrail were taken.

Photographs showing that the leaves obscured the walking surface were taken.
