NASA Lesson #0098: Displacement Leak Detector

Lesson ID: #0098 (Database ID: 98)
Date: 1992-08-05
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

A technician was performing leak checks at ports on the right side of the orbiter (OV-099) using a displacement leak detector. The tygon hose became tangled and the leak detector fell and hit the side of the vehicle damaging seven tiles. The detector was damaged when it finally hit the floor. The bay manager, safety, and the operations desk were notified of the incident, and the area was cleaned of spilled fluid & debris.

Lesson(s) Learned

Failure to require tethering of a flow meter used to perform leak checks resulted in damage to the orbiter when the meter fell and hit the orbiter.

Recommendation(s)

Add handles and tethering aids to the flow meters prior to use in or around the orbiter.
