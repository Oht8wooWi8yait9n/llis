NASA Lesson #1408: Project Termination & Transition

Lesson ID: #1408 (Database ID: 1408)
Date: 2004-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Due to economic reasons, the Project was no longer an investment the Agency wanted to make and was subsequently terminated prior to its completion. Therefore, the Project transitioned into a shutdown mode. However, the sudden and unplanned early termination did not leave the Project with adequate resources to accomplish a complete shutdown aimed at preserving the Agency's investment thus far (intellectual and real properties developed during the Project's run), and securing appropriate positions for its personnel. However, the Project Manger (PM) was able to find appropriate positions for his personnel, primarily from person-to-person advocacy but was himself tied up in addressing contractual issues with the Project's contractor and unable to transition into a new activity more than a year and a half after the termination.

Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Projects should have a termination plan in place to ensure that the Agency's investment is maximized. This is critical in cases where termination is premature (cases where a Project is ended prior to running its entire planned life-cycle). In particular, the following should be considered and included in the termination plan:

a. The amount of resources required to secure, document, and disseminate critical Project information (Lessons Learned, technical, administrative and management achievements)
b. The integration of Project personnel and other resources into appropriate Center activities once they are released from their Project assignments
c. The partnering, dependencies, and legal issues with respect to termination of the Project's agreements with other entities
d. Re-activation strategies and plans, should the Project or a closely related activity take up the Project's requirements in the future

Recommendation(s)

To ensure the Agency's investments are maximized after a Project termination (early or as scheduled), the following should be accomplished:

  1. Include a plan for transition and termination into the Project's planning documents that include all the items listed in the LL section above.
