NASA Lesson #1457: Accident Investigations/Archiving of Data & Library Requirements

Lesson ID: #1457 (Database ID: 1457)
Date: 2003-08-31
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

For the Columbia accident investigation, handling, destination and formats of archival documentation were not fully considered in initial startup of library requirements.

Lesson(s) Learned

For accident investigations, handle all data and documentation with the understanding that it will eventually be officially archived.

Recommendation(s)

  • Be aware that most data collected and archived by the accident investigation board and NASA task force will be sent to the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) and will be consider public information unless directed otherwise.
  • Segregate and mark all export controlled, sensitive, and proprietary data accordingly before sending data to NARA.
  • Brief all investigators and staff on handling of proprietary and privileged information, specifically in the context of what we can and cannot put in the final report (public domain) and also in the context of archives and how they are handled.
  • Do not permit various IT formats to be delivered. Have the NASA Archivist recommend formats for delivering data (to include formats for photos). Native format (not PDF) may be best because it allows cutting and pasting. If PDF, documents must be text searchable (i.e., OCR or other).
  • Pre-establish e-mail archiving policy.
