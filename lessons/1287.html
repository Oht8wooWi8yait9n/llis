NASA Lesson #1287: Program Management's Lead Role in the Project's Risk Management Process

Lesson ID: #1287 (Database ID: 1287)
Date: 2002-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Program's senior management had a visible commitment to risk management, which was demonstrated by their attendance in specialized training and work with consultants. The Program followed through on their commitment by developing and implementing a Program Risk Management Plan. While the Program then levied the same risk management requirements and accountability on the project (each project was responsible for writing and implementing their own risk management plan), it also provided support and an example for the projects to follow thereby reducing their workload.

Lesson(s) Learned

Program-wide (across all projects) commitment and implementation consistency for new initiatives and/or existing practices is maximized when Program management shows active support and leadership. In addition, projects conserve resources when addressing new initiatives and/or existing practices by leveraging off the Program's accomplishments such that each does not have to start from scratch.

Recommendation(s)

New initiatives and existing practices such as risk management that are required from all Program elements should be supported and championed at the Program level to ensure Program-wide commitment. The Program should also provide examples, guidelines and templates, if applicable, to its projects to ensure program wide consistency in breadth and depth of coverage for new initiatives and/or existing practices.
