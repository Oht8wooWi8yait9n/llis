NASA Lesson #1314: Minimizing the Governments Risk by Sharing Program Cost and Responsibilities With the Contractor

Lesson ID: #1314 (Database ID: 1314)
Date: 2002-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The Program cost and responsibilities were split 50/50 between the Government and the Contractor. This arrangement reflected the interest that each party had; the Government was interested in integrating several new technologies into a flight demonstration vehicle while the Contractor was interested in showcasing its rapid prototyping capabilities. These complimentary interests ensured each party maintained their commitments and mutual support.

Lesson(s) Learned

It was extremely beneficial to have the Program responsibilities and financial commitments clearly defined such that they would be shared between the Government and Contractor. Sharing the "cost" of success or failure brought out the best in people and their organizations and kept them motivated.

Recommendation(s)

Contractual arrangements that place an equal amount of responsibility and accountability on the Contactor should be considered when formulating a program's agreements.
