NASA Lesson #0154: Pneumatic and Fluid Quick Disconnects.

Lesson ID: #0154 (Database ID: 154)
Date: 1992-10-08
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The quick disconnect/filter assemblies are designed to provide a disconnect interface and remove all solid particles which exceed 25 microns. The fluids being filtered include hydrazine (N2H4), GN2, lube oil, and water. The ground half QD assemblies interface the GSE at the ground umbilical carriers S70-0757 and S70-0758 which provide structural support and fluid containment. If the QD is mated incorrectly, it may cause the QD to leak N2H4 during fill/drain operations.



Lesson(s) Learned

The pneumatic and fliud quick disconnects are the same size and located close enough to easily allow misconnection within APU carrier plates.

Recommendation(s)

Replace all similar and same size connectors with keyed or different sized connectors.
