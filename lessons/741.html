NASA Lesson #0741: Hazards of Metal Desks

Lesson ID: #0741 (Database ID: 741)
Date: 2000-02-23
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

An employee severed the tip of his finger while unloading a metal desk from a truck. One of the desk's sheet metal leg covers had slid down the leg's support beam and created a pinch point between the sheet metal leg cover and the desktop, catching the employee's finger.

[D]

[D]

Lesson(s) Learned

The employee was a safety-conscious professional furniture mover and was wearing heavy leather gloves. Unfortunately, this was not enough protection from the unexpected sharp edge of the leg cover. When performing any task that involves furniture moving, be on the lookout for potential pinch point hazards and wear personal protective equipment.

A coworker took the employee to a local hospital for emergency treatment, although no one called the emergency numbers before leaving. It is important to ensure that employees are familiar with their facility emergency procedures and numbers.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Inspect furniture periodically. Identify possible pinch points, broken or loose parts, sharp edges, or other hazards.
  2. Report furniture that appears damaged or hazardous to the furniture repair department.
  3. Never move large furniture yourself. Call the moving and hauling department to schedule the rearrangement of furniture.
  4. Remember to call the appropriate emergency numbers for serious injuries.
