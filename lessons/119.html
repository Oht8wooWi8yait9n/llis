NASA Lesson #0119: Solid Rocket Motor (SRM) Segment and Handling Ring Mating

Lesson ID: #0119 (Database ID: 119)
Date: 1992-08-10
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

On September 25, 1987, The solid rocket motor (SRM) aft segment was being mated to the aft segment handling ring in the H77-0386 stand. The SRM segment and handling ring assembly was hoisted from the stand and moved to the breakover beam. The handling ring was mated to the breakover beam and the SRM segment was tilted to the horizontal position and placed on the rail car. At this time it was noticed that a total of six bolts were missing from the SRM end-ring assembly. It is unknown when or who removed the bolts.



Lesson(s) Learned

  1. Documentation lacking cross references between serial numbers and model numbers may result in inadequate configuration or missing hardware.
  2. Improper work authorization contributed to the unauthorized removal of six attachment bolts from the SRM aft segment lifting bracket.


Recommendation(s)

  1. Implement documentation to allow work to be traced to hardware serial numbers and personnel performing the task.
  2. Revise documentation to include hardware inspections and to correct discrepencies for all transportation support equipment transferred.
