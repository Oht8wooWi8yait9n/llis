NASA Lesson #1313: Tailoring Project Management Review and Approval Processes Based on Project Requirements

Lesson ID: #1313 (Database ID: 1313)
Date: 2002-01-01
Organization: Ames Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

In addition to "normal" tight budgets, schedules and technical requirements, the Project had several constraints and conditions that posed additional challenges including:

  1. Small "one person deep" team
  2. Multi-institutional team consisting of Government and Industry
  3. Multiple sites (test vehicles assembled at Contractors site were transported to Dryden Flight Research Center for operation)
  4. Classified security requirements
The Project used these challenges to streamline its processes by tailoring the existing Government and Contractor procedures, polices, and requirements to fit the specific needs of the Project.

Lesson(s) Learned

As a result of streamlining its processes to fit the its specific needs and constraints, the Project experienced the following benefits:

  1. Fewer but highly efficient and effective reviews
  2. Efficient and effective distribution of authority/decisions
  3. Minimization of management overhead
  4. Efficient and effective use of small Project team

Recommendation(s)

During a projects formulation a plan for tailoring its implementation and operational processes should be developed. The plan should address how the project will integrate and tailor existing Government and Contractor processes to meet the project's specific needs. The automatic and rigid use of boilerplate processes should be avoided.
