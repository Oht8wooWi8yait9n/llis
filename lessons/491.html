NASA Lesson #0491: High -Voltage Hazard

Lesson ID: #0491 (Database ID: 491)
Date: 1996-12-24
Organization: Johnson Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Support contractor personnel contacted high voltage with a paint scraping knife while preparing a cable tray for painting in a facility / building transformer enclosure, causing a flash fire. Two of the worker¹s clothing caught fire and nearby combustibles were ignited. Two workers sustained burns to their hands and faces when one of the workers inserted the knife in a vent/drain slot. This accident did not result in permanent disability or death but the potential is evident.

Reference(s): NASA JSC Safe Alert dated, 11/01/95

Lesson(s) Learned

It is imperative that the workers be fully informed of the hazards of the energized electrical systems before working around them.

Recommendation(s)

  1. Conduct physical inspections of all work areas to identify hazardous areas and operations.
  2. Closely control all restricted access areas to prevent mishaps which might be precipitated by untrained individuals.
  3. All personnel must be knowledgeable and fully capable of understanding the full meaning and message intended by caution, warning, and danger signs.
  4. Take time to review the risks of a job or work site before approving or beginning any job.
  5. Ensure proper procedures have been provided to address hazardous operations and involved personnel are aware of the procedures and all identified hazards.
  6. Never work on or near energized equipment unless you have the proper training and protective equipment.
