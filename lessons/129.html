NASA Lesson #0129: The Oxygen Vent System.

Lesson ID: #0129 (Database ID: 129)
Date: 1992-08-24
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: None
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

The vacuum servicing units provide the capability to evacuate the PRSD tanks and the relief line. The unit consists of a skid mounted frame, electrical and fluid control panels and an electric motor driven vacuum pump. If the vacuum servicing unit is left connected to the suction line, inadvertent opening of manual valve A81992 could subject the pump to 935 psig from the cryo valve panel. This is beyond the unit's capability and could expel pump oil through the vent system.



Lesson(s) Learned

Failure to protect the vacuum servicing unit from inadvertent actuations of the 935 psig cryo valve panel could result in damage to flight hardware.

Recommendation(s)

Install a pressure actuated check valve to prevent the high cryo pressure from affecting the vacuum servicing unit.
