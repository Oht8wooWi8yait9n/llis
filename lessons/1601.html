NASA Lesson #1601: Article damage during testing/Test procedure deviation

Lesson ID: #1601 (Database ID: 1601)
Date: 2005-06-02
Organization: Armstrong Flight Research Center
Program Phase: None
Mission Directorate: Aeronautics Research, Human Exploration and Operations, Science
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

During an approved test procedure, an additional effort was undertaken in the hopes of saving time, and expediting a modification. This modification was not approved as part of the test configuration. Not fully understanding the sequence of the test routine, the wing and rudder came into contact resulting in minor to moderate damage to both. Though an engineering solution prevented the need to replace the rudder, the schedule impact was not trivial while determining the extent of the damage and adequacy of the solution.

Lesson(s) Learned

Deviations in approved testing procedures, no matter how insignificant, can result in oversights and assumptions that could lead to serious project schedule and cost impacts.

Recommendation(s)

Follow approved testing procedures without deviation.
