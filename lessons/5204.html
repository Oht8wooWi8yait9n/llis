NASA Lesson #5204: Enterprise Architecture /Access to Program Data

Lesson ID: #5204 (Database ID: 5204)
Date: 2011-01-12
Organization: Kennedy Space Center
Program Phase: Approval
Mission Directorate: Human Exploration and Operations
Topic / Category: Systems Engineering and Analysis, Information Technology / Systems, Engineering design and project processes and standards
Source: View Official NASA LLIS Entry (live system)

Driving Event / Description

Constellation teams were spread out among many NASA Centers, locations, and time zones and were sometimes unable to access the program data they needed. Team members had difficulty accessing documents, joining telecons, and viewing review-related presentations, which caused them to miss important discussions and delayed document reviews.

Lesson(s) Learned

The Constellation Program would have benefited from an enterprise architecture that provided the applications and technologies to adequately disseminate information to program team members at multiple locations.

Recommendation(s)

In the business models for future programs, include a program-centric dashboard (interactive user interface) that gives the program team members access to the tools and information they need to perform their work. This dashboard can provide visibility into program-wide activities and promote collaboration between team members.
